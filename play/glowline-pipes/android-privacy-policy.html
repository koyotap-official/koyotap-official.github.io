Privacy Policy for GlowLine Pipes (Android)

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy applies specifically to GlowLine Pipes for Android (package name: com.koyotap.glowlinepipes), provided by KoyoTap. This English version is the governing text.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, settings, your selected age range (or an unexpired age group carried forward from a previous version) and its confirmation time, and the ownership state of the Ad-Free Pack on your device. Age records expire one year after confirmation. Game progress and settings are not sent to KoyoTap servers and are not synchronized between devices. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes this local information. A previously purchased Ad-Free Pack can be restored through Google Play.

Age check and advertising

GlowLine Pipes is intended for users of all ages. Before initializing the advertising SDK, the app shows a neutral age-check screen with two choices: under 12, or 12 and older. The app does not ask for a date of birth. The selected range and confirmation time are stored locally for up to one year. Neither choice establishes that a user is 21 or older, so both use restricted advertising settings and are not offered rewarded video ads. An unexpired adult age check from a previous app version may remain active until its original one-year expiry. Ad content is capped at a General (G) rating.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Users who have not purchased the Ad-Free Pack may see automatic interstitial ads. The one-time Ad-Free Pack removes those automatic interstitial ads. Optional rewarded video ads are available only while a valid adult age check from the previous app version remains on the device.

To provide, measure, and protect advertising services, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share an IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers. These data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention or security. Ad SDK communications are encrypted in transit. Where Google's consent management flow is required, the app checks consent before requesting ads and provides the available privacy-options form. See the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play purchases

The Ad-Free Pack is processed through Google Play Billing. The app reads the product identifier and purchase status to grant or restore the ad-removal entitlement. Purchase tokens are used with Google Play to acknowledge purchases and are not sent to KoyoTap servers. Google Play handles payment details such as card information; KoyoTap does not access those details. Information processed by Google Play is subject to Google's payments and purchase services.

Information KoyoTap receives

The app does not require an account and does not send game progress, the age group, or purchase tokens to a KoyoTap backend. Optional usage analytics use Google Analytics with the separate permission described below. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message to respond.

Deleting information

You can remove local game data and the locally stored age group by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. KoyoTap does not maintain an account or server-side game profile for this app. Google handles information processed by its advertising and payment services under its own policies and controls.

Optional usage analytics and referral measurement

This section applies to Android versions that provide the optional usage analytics setting described below. It does not apply to older versions or iOS builds without that setting.

The app stores short source, medium, campaign, and content tags extracted from Google Play install referral information, together with the original installation time, on your device. It does not store or send the full referral URL query. Keeping this local record does not by itself send data to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics for Firebase is enabled only after you confirm that you are at least 18 and choose to allow optional usage analytics. It is disabled before permission and for users under 18 or whose age has not been confirmed. You can play without allowing it. This choice is separate from advertising consent; consenting to ads does not enable these analytics.

If you allow analytics, the app sends the referral tags and original installation date and time (a UTC date and a time in seconds), an app instance identifier, app version, device and operating-system information, and basic events automatically collected by the SDK, such as first launches, sessions, and engagement, to Google. Google processes the IP address used for communications and may derive an approximate region. KoyoTap uses Google Analytics reports to understand which referrals lead to installations and to improve the app. The original installation date may differ from the date an event is sent after permission.

Android advertising ID collection is disabled for these Analytics measurements, and consent for advertising data use and advertising personalization remains denied. We do not add your name, email address, precise location, purchase tokens, or payment details to these analytics. The advertising and purchase sections of this policy describe information separately processed by Google Mobile Ads and Google Play.

You can withdraw permission in the app's settings at any time to stop subsequent Analytics collection. Withdrawal or uninstalling the app does not immediately delete information already sent to Google. Previously sent information is subject to Google Analytics retention and deletion mechanisms. Contact us using the details at the end of this policy for questions about information handling or deletion. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.
